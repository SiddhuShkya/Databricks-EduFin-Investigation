# EduFin Capital Portfolio Risk Analysis - Video Script

*Estimated delivery time: 3–5 minutes*

### What case I worked on?

---

I investigated the **EduFin Capital Portfolio Risk Analysis** case. Triggered by a ₹12 Crore loan disbursement discrepancy affecting 15,000 student accounts, I had just 48 hours to audit the portfolio and deliver a board-ready remediation strategy ahead of a federal compliance audit.

### What I was trying to understand?

---

I set out to understand the macro portfolio health, identify which cities were losing the most capital, prioritize defaulting borrowers for targeted collections, and uncover which partner institutions were driving the high default rates. Ultimately, I needed to locate the root causes of our systemic exposure and identify actionable, risk-based interventions.

### What I ended up understanding from the case?

---

Analyzing the data revealed a systemic failure: our default rate is 11.80% (₹24.24 Crores lost), nearly double the industry benchmark, with 17.26% of the portfolio at risk. Geographically, Pune and Ranchi are critical default hotspots. On the customer level, poor credit screening allowed serial defaulters like Raghav Dubey (CIBIL 486) to borrow repeatedly. Institutionally, 13 partner institutes had default rates above 15%—led by State Commerce Institute at 20.87% default rate and ₹83.96 Crores in default exposure—while National Law Institute maintained a flawless 0% default rate across 10,000+ loans.

### What decisions my analysis led to?

---

My findings led to three critical decisions: first, immediately halting lending in high-risk cities (Ranchi, Pune, and Lucknow) and phasing out originations in four others; second, shifting the Collections team to a risk-prioritized call list to target high-risk debtors and escalate chronic defaulters to recovery agencies; and third, blacklisting the 13 underperforming partner institutions while studying and replicating the successful student screening model of the National Law Institute.

### What still needs improvement & further investigation?

---

We must still audit the source of the initial ₹12 Crore disbursement discrepancy to rule out internal fraud. Additionally, the credit scoring and verification models need an immediate overhaul to block high-risk borrowers. Finally, we need a solid transition framework for servicing active loans from blacklisted partner institutions, and a deep-dive investigation into the operational differences between institutes and universities to mitigate future institutional risk.
