Phase 4 — Institutional Partnership Risk: Which Partners Are Costing Us?

THURSDAY

Thursday Mindset Unlock

Today's output has legal weight. A recommendation to suspend a partnership affects contracts, relationships, and revenue. That means: you cannot recommend a suspension based on insufficient data. An institution with 3 defaults and 10 total loans has a 30% default rate — but that 30% is meaningless. Your job today is to be precise, not dramatic. Volume gates protect the business from acting on noise.

PARTNERSHIP DIRECTOR'S AUDIT — "Which institutions should we cut?"

Some partner institutions are consistently sending EduFin borrowers who default. The partnership director needs a clear, legally defensible list of institutions to: suspend, put on review, retain, or reward — based on their actual default performance. Generic rankings are not enough. Each decision must be justified.

CFO Email - Strategic Clarification

From: Rajesh Malhotra, CFO
To: Data Analytics Team
Date: 5 hours ago

Quick reminder as you analyze partner performance: our goal is maximizing portfolio profitability, not just minimizing default rates. A partner generating ₹50 Cr in disbursements with 15% defaults (₹7.5 Cr loss) still contributes ₹42.5 Cr net - that's better than a 'safe' partner with ₹10 Cr and 3% defaults (₹9.7 Cr net).

Factor in revenue impact before recommending any partnership suspensions.

STEP 4A — Open Metric Design: "Partner Health Score"

What you're doing: The Partnership Director wants to avoid "binary" decisions (Suspend/Retain). They want a score. You must define the components of this score using SQL math.

STEP 4B — Default Rate Per Institution

What you're doing: Calculate the default rate for each institution. Sort by rate descending. This is the starting point — but remember, the ending point is a multi-criteria decision, not a single ranking.
Note at least 2 institutions where rate and financial impact tell different stories. This contrast is the core insight of Thursday.

STEP 4C — Financial Loss Attribution

What you're doing: Calculate total defaulted loan value per institution in Crores. This converts performance rate into financial cost — the number that justifies partnership decisions to the CFO.
Which institution has the highest defaulted exposure in Crores? Is it the same institution with the highest default rate?

STEP 4D — Minimum Data Threshold Gate

What you're doing: Identify institutions with insufficient data — fewer than 50 loans. These must be explicitly excluded from the suspension recommendation and flagged as "Insufficient Data." This is statistical discipline, not leniency.
Write down: how many institutions are excluded by the 50-loan threshold? If you lowered it to 20 — would any new institutions enter the suspension candidate list?

STEP 4E — Partnership Review Dashboard (Final Output)

What you're doing: Combine all metrics into one output per institution: total loans, default rate, defaulted exposure, partner_flag (Suspend/Review/Retain/Reward), and minimum_threshold_flag. This is the final document the Partnership Director takes to the contract renegotiation meeting.
Read your top suspended institution's row aloud. Would a legal team accept this as grounds for contract suspension? Does it include: institution ID, rate, volume, exposure, and threshold exceeded?

BUILD YOUR CUSTOM COLUMNS — Required for Thursday Submission

NEW TECHNIQUE — Correlated Subqueries: Phase 1 = CASE. Phase 2 = Window Functions. Phase 3 = CTEs. Today you unlock correlated subqueries — a subquery that references a column from the outer query. This lets you compare each institution against the portfolio-wide baseline row by row.

Column 1 — partner_flag: Compare each institution's default rate against the portfolio-wide average default rate using a correlated subquery. Suspend where default_rate > 1.5× portfolio average AND total_loans >= 50. Review where > 1.0× average. Retain where below average. Reward where < 0.5× average AND total_loans >= 50.

Column 2 — anomaly_flag: Use EXISTS to flag institutions where at least one borrower has CIBIL > 900 (data anomaly). This is cross-table validation. Mark as: "Data Quality Review Required" or "Data Clean".

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT — Answer These Three Questions

1. The institution with 0 defaults — what action did you assign it, and why? Write your classification AND your reasoning. ("I assigned it X because Y, not Z because A.")
2. Write your Suspension Justification for the top-ranked institution to be suspended. Format it as if going to a legal review: Institution name, default rate, loan count, total defaulted exposure, which threshold was exceeded, recommended action, and what monitoring must happen post-suspension.
3. If you were the Partnership Director and received your final output — what is the FIRST call you make? Who do you call, about which institution, and what specifically do you say in that call?
